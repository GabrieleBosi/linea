## The override mechanism and why it exists

Nine events can be forced with `force: true` and a reason, the list in `src/domain/override.ts`: accept, reject, withdraw and reopen on a line; feasible and not feasible on its check; hold, reject and reopen on the request. Events that create rows of their own (quote, request_check, propose_alternative, supersede, convert) cannot be forced, because forcing them would move a status without the row it refers to. The event is written with `type = override`, the guard and the role check are skipped, and the design pages count the overrides. This is how the product learns the real process: the process has informal rules that nobody has written down, and every override proposes a new row in the transition table and a new test. Waiving a feasibility check is also logged as an override. The override itself is a P1 feature in the UI; the domain supports it from the first release so that the traces are complete.
