## The problem

Quoting configurable steel is a negotiation that lives in email threads, spreadsheets and people's memory. Years of past quotations exist, but at the moment a price is set nobody can find the three that matter. And nobody can say, today, what still stands between a request and an order.

## The one model

The important object is the request line, not the request. Each line has two independent tracks. The commercial track answers: is the price agreed? The technical track answers: is the configuration feasible? A line is executable when both tracks are complete. A request becomes an executable order when every open line is executable. That one idea covers partial acceptance, late feasibility problems, changes and mixed states without special cases.
