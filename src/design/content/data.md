## The generator's assumptions

About 250 legacy quotations with dates from January 2023 to September 2026, made with a deterministic random generator so that every run gives the same rows. Twelve customers: four anchor accounts plus eight more, each with a favorite family and two favorite sizes so that references cluster like real accounts. Products are HEA, HEB and IPE from the catalog. Materials are S355 at 70 percent, S460 at 25 percent and S235 at 5 percent. Lengths 6 to 15 metres, weighted to 12 metres; the length is not stored, as in a typical legacy table. Quantities 20 to 300 pieces, log-normal around 100. The production cost comes from the same cost model the product uses, plus 8 percent noise. Margins come from a normal distribution with mean 19.5 percent and standard deviation 2.2 points, clipped to 14 to 26 percent; the quoted price is the cost divided by one minus the margin, rounded to 10. Revisions 1 to 4 with weights 30, 40, 20 and 10 percent. The outcome is won with a probability that falls with the margin and the revision count and rises for the customer's favorite family.

## The calibration test

Three tests run on every commit. For each of the eight anchor rows, the cost model computes a cost for each length from 6 to 15 metres; the anchor's production cost must sit inside that range. It does, for all eight. The overall win rate of the generated history must sit between 60 and 75 percent, and across the whole history, all families together, the win rate must fall from one margin band to the next. The trend holds overall; it does not hold in every family or every small band. The top band of one family can rest on a handful of quotations, and a handful is too few to show a rate: in HEA the 23 to 26 percent band can read higher than the band below it. ## The eight anchor rows

Eight quotations are set by hand to anchor the history: the two scenario customers and two more accounts, with figures chosen so that the calibration holds (six of eight won, three of four in the 17 to 20 percent band) and scenario A finds its references. They carry `source = given`. Every other row has `source = generated`.
